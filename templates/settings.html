{# Import #}

Import data

Upload a CSV of completed trades.

{% call alert(variant='info', title='First-time setup order') %}
  1. Import your CSV of historical completed trades first.
  2. Go to Portfolio and deposit your starting capital (the amount you began trading with).
  3. Date that deposit before your earliest trade (e.g. if first trade is 01/01/2024, deposit on 31/12/2023).
  4. Add current open positions via Manual entry.
  5. Your final cash = Starting capital + Historical P&L − Open positions.
{% endcall %}
Maximum 10 MB.
{# CSV format guide - native disclosure #}
{{ icon('help-circle', size=14) }} CSV format guide

Headers are matched without regard to case or spaces: Entry Price reads as entry_price.

Required columns
  • date — entry date, YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY
  • symbol
  • direction — Long or Short
  • entry_price, exit_price, quantity
Recommended
  • exit_date — same formats; defaults to the entry date if omitted
  • entry_time/time, exit_time — HH:MM; give both for an exact holding time
  • trade_style or position_type — Intraday/Swing/Investment (defaults to Swing)
Other optional
  • market, charges, leverage
  • stop_loss, take_profit, risk_amount
  • strategy, setup_type, market_conditions
  • notes, tags, mistakes

Each row creates paired entry + exit executions. Holding time is counted from the dates, or from the entry and exit times when both are given.
